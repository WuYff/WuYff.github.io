COCOA: Action-Conditioned Correction for Vision-Language-Action Policies

Yi Wu, Manav Kulshrestha, Junyi Wu, Kai Cheng, Nan Jiang, Aniket Bera, Lin Tan

A separate corrective policy that revises the action chunks proposed by a frozen flow-based VLA before execution, trained with supervised flow matching followed by correction preference optimization. Consistent gains on SimplerEnv, CALVIN, and real-robot tasks across π0.5, FLOWER VLA, and Xiaomi-Robotics-0.

Preprint 2026

Analyzing and Mitigating Cost-Inefficient Behaviors in Coding Agents

Yiran Hu, Nan Jiang, Shanchao Liang, Anik Dey, Yi Wu, Lin Tan

A study of 1,200 Claude Code and Mini-SWE-Agent trajectories on SWE-bench that identifies three recurring cost-inefficient behaviors, then shows developer-designed skills cut task cost by up to 41.73%, roughly twice the gain of agent-synthesized skills.

ICRA 2025 Best Paper Award Finalist

SELP: Generating Safe and Efficient Task Plans for Robot Agents with Large Language Models

Yi Wu, Zikang Xiong, Yiran Hu, Shreyash S. Iyengar, Nan Jiang, Aniket Bera, Lin Tan, Suresh Jagannathan

Equivalence voting over LTL formulas, LTL-constrained decoding, and domain-specific fine-tuning make LLM planners follow complex user constraints, improving safety rate by 10.8% and plan efficiency by 19.8% on drone navigation.
